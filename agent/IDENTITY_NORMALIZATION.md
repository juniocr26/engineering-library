# Identity, provenance and retention

The owner's 2026-11-16 extended-campaign instruction supersedes the earlier requirement to keep raw experiments in this versionable tree. Historical transformation reports, inventories, checkpoints, source excerpts, requests, responses and media remain recoverable in access-controlled, Git-ignored operational storage. Their bytes, modes and historical hashes were verified before relocation. The original transformation report is preserved there; this file is current guidance, not a rewritten historical outcome.

The active agent contains reusable instructions, the three approved skill contexts, their upstream license/provenance and separately identified draft authoring/evaluation examples. Those examples remain unapproved review material. The actual caller loads only the three skill contexts; it does not implicitly load source manifests, examples, expected answers or historical outcomes.

Before retiring experimental material, inventory its ownership, sensitive content, legal notices, references and recovery needs. Preserve a verified private copy first. Do not discard evidence to conceal its origin, overwrite historical hashes or turn failures into successes. Preserve required third-party attribution with its associated content. Active skill attribution remains in [LICENSE.upstream](skills/LICENSE.upstream) and [provenance.json](skills/provenance.json).

Neutral case names and symbolic `case-source://` references do not establish anonymity, source freshness, publication readiness or an executable installation. Original project documentation keeps correct identities. A reusable example requires review of its text, paths, metadata, images, archive members and provenance; never publish a reversible real-name-to-case mapping. Private operational originals remain separate from sanitized candidates.

Historical source/request/capture hashes identify the historical bytes specified by their original ledgers. Identity-normalized copies have their own hashes and must not be presented as untouched originals or as code executed after transformation. Preserve both distinctions in the private archive. Current guidance is not covered by an old inventory merely because its path is unchanged.

Previously committed identifying content remains in Git history. No history rewriting, staging, commits, pushes or publication was performed. A future destructive history operation requires separate owner authorization.
